Privacy Policy

Updated: 17 October 2026

Information processed

When you register, Supabase Authentication processes your email address, credentials, account identifier and related authentication information. FlixLeo saves display-name and language preferences, watchlists and playback progress linked to your account. Some playback preferences and history can also be stored on your device.

How it is used

We use account information to authenticate you and keep your watchlist and Continue Watching available between devices. Movie and TV metadata and external playback sources may involve third-party services with their own policies.

Storage and security

Account data is held through Supabase and restricted by account-level database access policies. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Passwords are handled by Supabase Authentication, not saved in FlixLeo's watchlist table.

Your choices

Account settings allow you to change your profile and password. You may request access to or deletion of your account information from the site operator. Signing out does not automatically erase saved cloud information.

Sharing and playback controls

Movie and series sharing uses the device share sheet to send a title name and a FlixLeo link to an app you choose. Playback controls, including aspect ratio, subtitle selection, subtitle uploads, synchronization and player visibility preferences, are processed on the device. Uploaded subtitle files may be temporarily cached on your device for playback; external providers may process content when you use their services.

Policy changes

Updates will be posted here as the service evolves.
