Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · FlixLeo by Santh Creatives

This policy describes information processed when you use the FlixLeo website, native Android app or desktop experience.

Information processed

If you create an account, FlixLeo processes your email address, authentication details, profile preferences and saved watchlists. The app may save playback progress, selected server and device-related session information so you can resume viewing or authorize a TV. Technical request records may be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers.

How it is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, protect sessions, show personalized catalogues, synchronize permitted watchlists and progress, and operate the service. Stream providers, metadata providers and externally opened links may process information under their own policies.

Storage and retention

Some preferences and viewing progress are stored locally on your device; signed-in information may be stored through hosted authentication and application databases. Retention depends on the data type and operational needs. Uninstalling an app does not automatically delete account information held on a server.

Your choices

You can sign out, manage available profile preferences and request information or account deletion through the contact details provided by FlixLeo. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in profile text.

Security

We use access controls and security measures appropriate to the service, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options available at flixleo.cyou.
